# Sunfeed PPA: Clause Review (Patna Circle, Package-18)

Source: `PPA Package-18 patna sunfeed c.docx`
Agreement: Contract-cum-PPA for 6 MW(DC) / 5585 Kutir Jyoti rooftop plants (1.1 kW each), CAPEX-cum-RESCO, 10 years, between Sunfeed Ecosolutions India Pvt Ltd (JV with Jai Auto Vehicles Pvt Ltd) as SPD and SBPDCL/NBPDCL (DISCOMs).

## 0. Read this first

**Assumption about your position.** The repo (work orders, JCR tracking, RMS reports, installation register) shows you are a vendor/installer working for Sunfeed under work orders. "Against Sunfeed" is therefore read as: clauses you can cite to (a) hold Sunfeed to payment and cooperation duties, (b) resist Sunfeed passing DISCOM-side risk down to you, and (c) spot where Sunfeed itself is exposed and may try to pass that exposure to you. If you meant something else (for example, you are on the DISCOM side), tell me and I will re-cut the file.

**Limits of this review.**
- The .docx uses automatic numbering, so extracted text has no numbers. Clause numbers below come from the document's own cross-references (4.4, 4.5, 9.5, 12.3.8, etc.). Where a number is my inference it is marked `(inferred)`. Verify against the signed copy.
- The PPA is between Sunfeed and the DISCOMs. You are not a party. Your rights against Sunfeed come from your own work orders/subcontract, not from this PPA. The PPA is useful as evidence of what Sunfeed promised, what it gets paid, and what risk it carries.
- This is a commercial reading, not legal advice. Have counsel review before sending any notice.

**Derived numbers (for negotiation).**
- 5585 plants × ₹43,000 (CFA + SFA) = **₹24.01 crore** of capex-side receipts (CFA ₹18.43 Cr + SFA ₹5.59 Cr).
- Per plant at 125 kWh/month (the stated 15.5% CUF cap) × ₹2.95 = ₹368.75/month ≈ **₹44,250 over 10 years**.
- 1.1 kW × 8760 h × 15% = ~1,445 kWh/year (~120 kWh/month), the minimum CUF level.

## 7. Suggested modifications

### 7.1 To your work orders / subcontract with Sunfeed (most actionable)

1. **Payment trigger.** Pay within a fixed number of days (for example 15 or 30) of plant-wise JCR submission. Not conditional on DISCOM approval, CFA release, or Sunfeed receipts. Reason: Sunfeed receives CFA directly from MNRE and SFA on fixed dates (Art. 9.1), so its cash does not depend on tariff payments.
2. **Late payment interest.** Add interest (for example 12–18% p.a.) on any delayed vendor payment. The PPA itself has no late-payment surcharge (item 5.12), so the interest cost would otherwise be zero for Sunfeed.
3. **Back-to-back, but only for what you control.** Flow down LDs only for delays caused solely by the vendor, capped at a percentage of the affected work order value. Do not reference PBG-based LDs (item 4.1).
4. **Excluded delay events (EOT list).** Incorporate PPA Art. 4.4 and 4.2 events: late consumer list, no consent, roof access refusal (8 AM–6 PM), no import-export meter, DISCOM dispatch instruction pending, vendor approval pending, GTP/PERT approval pending, Force Majeure. Link to the hindrance register as the proof standard (item 3.5).
5. **Documentation of hindrance.** Contractually require Sunfeed or its authorized representative to countersign hindrance entries within a set number of days, with deemed acceptance if not countersigned.
6. **Material responsibility.** State that DCR, ALMM, BIS, PDI and vendor-approval compliance rests with Sunfeed for materials it supplies. Vendor is responsible only for workmanship and materials it procures itself.
7. **Warranty split.** Workmanship warranty period fixed in the work order (do not accept an unspecified "as per PPA"). Material warranties pass through from OEM. Sunfeed's 10-year inverter promise (Art. 9.4) is not extended to you.
8. **O&M scope.** If you will perform O&M, sign a separate O&M order with price, SLA, spares provision, access assumptions, and a clear start and end date. Do not accept the PPA's 10-year O&M by reference.
9. **Retention and holdbacks.** Limit retention to a fixed percentage and release it on a defined date or event. Do not mirror the PPA's 20% holdback (Art. 9.2) unless the cause is yours.
10. **Joint and several exposure.** Add a clause that you are not liable for Sunfeed/Jai Auto JV defaults or DISCOM blacklisting events (Art. 12.3.8). Add a right to suspend and terminate for non-payment.
11. **Change in law / scheme.** Include a price-adjustment clause for changes in MNRE/CFA rules, since the tariff is fixed with no escalation (Art. 9.3) and the PPA passes no compensation for such changes.
12. **Data and records access.** Ensure you hold copies of the commissioning certificates, JCRs, photos and portal acknowledgments for each plant. These are your evidence of completion and the trigger for Sunfeed's CFA.

### 7.2 Clarifications to request from Sunfeed (and through it, DISCOM)

1. Corrected cross-references (items 5.1–5.5).
2. Which LD formula is intended in Art. 4.3 (item 5.6), and whether the 1 MW example applies per plant or per package.
3. End-of-term transfer: consumers or DISCOM (item 5.8)?
4. Complete text of the JV default clause 12.1.1(vii) (item 5.10).
5. Whether DISCOM acknowledgment of the hard-copy bill is deemed after a fixed number of days (item 5.11).
6. Written site list, consent status, and dispatch instruction dates for each lot (items 3.1, 3.2).

### 7.3 Amendments Sunfeed itself could seek from the DISCOMs (useful if you need Sunfeed to be flexible)

1. Add late-payment surcharge for DISCOM delay.
2. Add deemed acknowledgment of bills after N days.
3. Rationalise the PBG-based LD to per-plant LD.
4. Allow replacement-site substitution for non-consenting consumers within the contracted capacity before the SCD.
5. Fix numbering and cross-references so default and dispute clauses are enforceable.

## 8. Evidence checklist to start keeping now

- Date the DISCOM consumer list was received.
